# Accepted Loan Status Business Interpretation

This document translates the accepted-loan EDA `loan_status` values into business language for repayment-risk analysis. It is based on the full accepted LendingClub dataset, not only the 250,000-row EDA working sample.

## Final Modeling Decision

The final binary modeling pipeline uses a strict completed-loan target:

| `loan_status` | Final target treatment | `target_bad` |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `Fully Paid` | Good terminal outcome | `0` |
| `Charged Off` | Bad terminal outcome | `1` |

All other statuses are excluded from the strict binary training target because they are unresolved, rare, policy-exception cases, or delinquency snapshots rather than the clean two-outcome business definition used in the final modeling notebooks.

This strict definition is consistent with the cleaning and preprocessing outputs consumed by the numbered modeling notebooks.

## Broader Business Decision Context

For binary repayment/default modeling, use only loans with sufficiently observed outcomes. Do not treat active or newly issued loans as good outcomes just because they have not failed yet.

- `Fully Paid` statuses are low observed repayment risk and can be treated as good terminal outcomes.
- `Charged Off` statuses are high observed repayment risk and are treated as bad terminal outcomes in the final model.
- `Default`, late delinquency statuses, and policy-exception statuses indicate elevated risk, but they are excluded from the strict final training target to keep the label stable and auditable.
- `Current`, `Issued`, and `In Grace Period` are unresolved or not fully mature outcomes. They should not be labeled as low risk in a binary default model.

## Key Interpretation

The accepted-loan dataset is imbalanced, but the issue is not `loan_status` missingness. `loan_status` is missing for only 33 rows, or 0.0015% of the full dataset.

The main modeling issue is censoring: 38.8515% of the full dataset is `Current`. These loans are still active and have not yet become either fully paid or charged off/defaulted. Labeling them as good loans would create target bias.

For the final supervised model, the clean target population is restricted to `Fully Paid` and `Charged Off`. This gives a stable binary target aligned with the numbered preprocessing and modeling notebooks:

```text
target_bad = 0  -> Fully Paid
target_bad = 1  -> Charged Off
```

Late, default, grace-period, current, policy-exception, and missing statuses should remain documented for business interpretation, but they are not part of the strict final training label.
