Demo Position

The generator is a research prototype for adverse-action-style letters. It uses deterministic SHAP reason selection, then RAG retrieval from Regulation B, FCRA, CFPB circulars, and official appendices.

The final output should be described as an explanation bridge, not as a compliance-approved lending notice.

The RAG letters improved legal-detail scoring, but two of five still failed to name a real federal agency. A production workflow should make that agency block deterministic.

Pipeline

1

Corpus

Reg B, FCRA, CFPB circulars, Appendix A, Appendix C, and official commentary are saved and chunked.

2

Retrieval

BM25 and MiniLM dense retrieval are fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.

3

SHAP Reasons

High-risk borrower examples keep mapped risk-increasing features, capped at four reasons.

4

Generation

Gemini writes RAG and no-RAG letters using the same borrower reasons.

5

Judging

A separate Gemini judge scores statutory accuracy with a fixed 5-item rubric.

corpus/chunk_corpus.py -> corpus/chunks.jsonl retrieval/build_index.py -> retrieval/index/embeddings.npy generation/generate_all.py -> generation/output/letters.jsonl generation/judge.py -> generation/output/judged.jsonl
